# ⭐ Star Friends

A picture + audio learning game for little kids (ages 1–3, preschool to kindergarten).
Single player, no reading required, works in any modern browser. No build step, no server.

## How to play

Open `index.html` in a browser (Chrome, Edge, or Safari recommended for the voice).

1. **Make your character** — upload a photo, or pick an animal friend, or pick a shape.
2. **Tap "Let's Play!"** — this also turns the sound on.
3. **Pick a game** and answer by tapping the big pictures.

## Learning games

| Game | What kids practice |
|------|--------------------|
| 🔢 Counting | Counting objects and matching the number |
| 🎨 Colors | Recognizing colors |
| ⬛ Shapes | Recognizing shapes |
| 5️⃣ Numbers | Recognizing written numbers |
| 🐾 Animals | Recognizing animals (and their sounds at higher levels) |

## How kids advance

- Every correct answer earns a ⭐.
- A round is 5 questions. Getting all 5 right **levels up** that game (up to level 5).
- Higher levels add bigger numbers, more choices, and harder questions.
- Every correct answer pops a little firework; finishing a round sets off a
  full fireworks show with a "bang" and a celebration banner.

## Privacy

Everything stays on the device. An uploaded photo is shrunk and saved only in the
browser's local storage — it is never sent to any server. Progress (stars and levels)
is also saved locally.

## Files

- `index.html` — screens and layout
- `style.css` — kid-friendly styling and animations
- `game.js` — game logic, audio narration, fireworks, and saved progress
